# Claude + ElevenLabs — AI Voiceover Workflow (2026)

The Claude and ElevenLabs voiceover workflow is the fastest way to produce broadcast-quality AI audio content without a recording studio or voice actor. Claude writes the script — structured for natural spoken delivery, not for reading on a page — and ElevenLabs converts it to realistic voice audio in seconds. The combination works because the two tools are complementary: Claude's strength is crafting words that sound right when spoken aloud, and ElevenLabs' strength is making those words sound like a real human voice. This guide covers the exact prompts, ElevenLabs settings, and quality checks to produce voiceovers you can publish with confidence.
