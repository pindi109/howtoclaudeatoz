## The Mission

Nobody owns the definitive Claude AI reference. Existing content is scattered, surface-level, and rarely Claude-dedicated. This site exists to change that.

HowToClaudeAtoZ is built to be the first place any Claude user visits when they need to know how to do something — whether that is writing, SEO, automation, coding, business strategy, or anything in between. Every article is written to answer the question directly, with real examples, real workflows, and real results.

## What We Cover

The encyclopaedia is organised into eight content pillars:

1. **Getting Started with Claude** — For beginners and users switching from other AI tools
2. **Claude for Writing & Content** — Blog posts, copy, email, social media
3. **Claude for SEO** — Keyword research, content strategy, on-page optimisation
4. **Claude for Automation & AI Workflows** — Make.com, n8n, agents, APIs
5. **Claude for Video, Voice & Media** — Scripts, voiceovers, AI video production
6. **Claude Code & Developer Use Cases** — Coding workflows, debugging, documentation
7. **Claude for Business & Productivity** — Operations, strategy, customer service
8. **Advanced Claude** — Prompt engineering, model selection, deep dives

We also maintain a Comparison Pages section and a growing community Prompt Library.

## Transparency and Independence

HowToClaudeAtoZ is editorially independent. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Anthropic PBC, the company that develops Claude.
