# Affiliate Disclosure

**HowToClaudeAtoZ.com** — operated by Level up IT Solutions Ltd

*Last updated: 8 June 2026*

HowToClaudeAtoZ participates in affiliate marketing programmes. This page explains how those relationships work, which programmes we participate in, and how our editorial independence is maintained.

## What Are Affiliate Links?

Some links on this website are affiliate links. This means that if you click on a link and subsequently make a qualifying purchase or sign up for a service, Level up IT Solutions Ltd may receive a commission from the company at no additional cost to you.

Affiliate commissions help fund the ongoing development of this encyclopaedia and allow us to keep all content free to access.

## Compliance

This disclosure is provided in compliance with:

- **ASA (Advertising Standards Authority)** — UK guidelines on advertising and affiliate marketing
- **CAP Code** — UK Code of Non-broadcast Advertising and Direct & Promotional Marketing
- **FTC Guidelines** — US Federal Trade Commission guidelines on endorsements and testimonials (for US readers)
- **UK GDPR / PECR** — Cookie and tracking disclosures in our Privacy Policy

## How We Disclose Affiliate Links

We disclose affiliate relationships as follows:

- Articles containing affiliate links display a visible notice at the top of the page
- Affiliate links use `rel="noopener sponsored"` in the HTML, signalling their nature to search engines
- We do not publish sponsored posts or paid reviews

## Our Editorial Policy

Affiliate relationships **never** determine:

- Which tools we choose to cover
- The content or conclusions of our articles
- Our ratings, comparisons, or recommendations

We only recommend products and services that we believe are genuinely useful to Claude users. If we have a negative view of a product, we say so regardless of whether we have an affiliate relationship with it.

We do not accept payment for articles, positive reviews, or editorial coverage.

## Third-Party Tracking

When you click an affiliate link, the affiliate programme may set tracking cookies in your browser. These cookies allow the programme to attribute purchases to our referral. For more information on cookies, see our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/).
