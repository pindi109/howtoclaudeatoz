## 2. What Personal Data We Collect

### 2.1 Analytics Data (Google Analytics 4)

When you visit our website, we use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) to collect:

- Pages visited and time spent on each page
- Approximate geographic location (country and city)
- Device type, browser, and operating system
- Referring website (how you found us)
- Interaction events (clicks on affiliate links, search queries)

This data is collected via cookies and is processed by Google LLC, 1600 Amphitheatre Parkway, Mountain View, CA 94043, USA. Google may transfer this data to servers outside the UK. Google operates under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses for UK transfers.

**Legal basis:** Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f) UK GDPR) — to understand how our content is used and to improve the site. You may object to this processing at any time (see Section 6).

### 2.2 AI Chat Widget

Our site includes an AI assistant powered by Anthropic's Claude API. When you use the chat widget:

- Your typed messages are sent to our serverless function, which forwards them to Anthropic PBC's API
- The page title and topic context of the page you are on is included with each message
- Anthropic processes this data to generate a response
- We do not store chat messages on our servers
- Your chat history is held only in your browser session memory and is not transmitted to us

Anthropic PBC is located at 548 Market St PMB 90375, San Francisco, CA 94104-5401, USA. For Anthropic's data practices, see their [privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy).

**Legal basis:** Contract performance / legitimate interests — providing the AI assistant service you have requested.

### 2.3 GitHub Discussions and Issues

If you post in our GitHub Discussions (community forum) or submit a GitHub Issue (page suggestion, bug report), that data is processed by GitHub, Inc. (a subsidiary of Microsoft). GitHub's privacy policy applies to data processed through their platform.

### 2.4 Affiliate Link Clicks

When you click an affiliate link on our site, we record the click event via GA4 (program name and page URL). The affiliate program you visit will also set their own tracking cookies, governed by their own privacy policies.

### 2.5 Data You Provide Voluntarily

If you contact us directly by email or telephone, we will process the information you provide solely to respond to your enquiry. We do not add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

## 3. Cookies

We use the following categories of cookies:

| Cookie type | Purpose | Examples | Consent required? |

| Essential | Site functionality (session state) | Session cookies | No |

## 6. Your Rights Under UK GDPR

As a UK resident, you have the following rights:

- **Right of access** — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
- **Right to rectification** — ask us to correct inaccurate data
- **Right to erasure** — ask us to delete your personal data ("right to be forgotten")
- **Right to restriction** — ask us to limit how we use your data
- **Right to data portability** — receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format
- **Right to object** — object to processing based on legitimate interests, including for direct marketing
- **Rights related to automated decision-making** — not to be subject to solely automated decisions that significantly affect you

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at **pindi@howtoclaudeatoz.com**. We will respond within 30 days. We may ask for proof of identity before processing your request.
