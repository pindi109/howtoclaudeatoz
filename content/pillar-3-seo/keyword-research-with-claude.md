# How to Do Keyword Research with Claude AI (2026)

Keyword research with Claude AI is not about replacing your SEO data tools — it is about making them 10x faster and more strategic. Claude excels at the thinking layer of keyword research: generating seed ideas from scratch, grouping related terms by search intent, spotting content gaps, and suggesting which angles to pursue first. What it cannot do is pull live search volume or competition data from Google. The winning workflow is a two-stage approach: use Claude to brainstorm and structure your keyword universe, then run the output through Mangools, Surfer SEO, or a similar tool to validate and prioritise.
