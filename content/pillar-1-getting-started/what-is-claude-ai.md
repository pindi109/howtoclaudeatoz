# What is Claude AI? Complete 2026 Guide

Claude AI is an artificial intelligence assistant built by Anthropic — one of the leading AI safety research companies in the world. What is Claude AI, exactly? It is a large language model (LLM) capable of understanding and generating human-level text across almost any task: writing, summarising, coding, reasoning, research, and conversation. Claude is available via claude.ai and through an API for developers. Unlike some competitors, Anthropic built Claude with a deliberate emphasis on safety, honesty, and avoiding harmful outputs — making it a strong choice for individuals, businesses, and developers who want a capable and trustworthy AI assistant.
